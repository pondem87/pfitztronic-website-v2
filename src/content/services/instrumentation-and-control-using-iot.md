IoT can make operational conditions visible and support more timely decisions. We help connect sensors, devices, and software around a defined monitoring or control requirement.

## What this can include

- Identifying the measurements required for an operational use case.
- Connecting instruments and devices to data collection systems.
- Dashboards, alerts, and integration with existing applications.
- Designing control workflows and appropriate operator oversight.

## How we approach the work

We assess the operating environment, connectivity, data needs, and consequences of device or network failure. A limited pilot can establish whether the proposed approach is suitable before wider deployment.

## What we work towards

An integrated view of the physical process, with clear responsibilities for device management, maintenance, and response to alerts. Control requirements are defined and validated for the specific operating context.
