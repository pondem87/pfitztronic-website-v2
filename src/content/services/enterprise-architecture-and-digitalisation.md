Digitalisation involves decisions about how the organisation works as well as the technology it uses. We help connect those decisions so individual projects contribute to a coherent direction.

## What this can include

- Views of current business, information, application, and technology environments.
- Identification of integration needs, duplication, and constraints.
- Target architecture and principles to guide solution choices.
- Transition roadmaps that account for dependencies and priorities.

## How we approach the work

We start with the outcomes the organisation wants to achieve and assess the current environment. We then describe the changes needed across processes, systems, and information, sequencing them into achievable steps.

## What we work towards

A clear architectural direction and a roadmap that supports coordinated delivery. Architecture artefacts are kept focused on decisions, communication, and implementation needs.
