Cloud services should support the way your applications and teams operate. We help plan deployments and establish the operational practices needed to manage them over time.

## What this can include

- Reviewing workload requirements and selecting a suitable deployment approach.
- Configuring application environments, networking, and access controls.
- Establishing deployment workflows, monitoring, and backup arrangements.
- Reviewing resource usage, costs, and operational responsibilities.

## How we approach the work

We begin with the application's requirements and the organisation's support capacity. Deployment decisions account for availability needs, data handling, recovery expectations, and budget.

## What we work towards

A documented cloud environment with repeatable deployment processes and visibility into its operation. Management and support activities are agreed as part of the service scope.
