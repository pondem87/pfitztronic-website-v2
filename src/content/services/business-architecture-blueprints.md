A business architecture blueprint provides a shared picture of how an organisation creates value. We help make the relationships between strategic objectives and everyday operations explicit.

## What this can include

- Business capability maps and value-stream views.
- Links between objectives, capabilities, information, and stakeholders.
- Identification of capability gaps and overlapping responsibilities.
- Blueprints that inform investment and change priorities.

## How we approach the work

We work with stakeholders to understand the current business, clarify intended outcomes, and describe the capabilities required for the future. The level of detail is chosen to support the decisions at hand.

## What we work towards

A practical set of business views that teams can use to discuss priorities and coordinate change. The blueprint serves as a decision aid and can evolve as the organisation's direction changes.
