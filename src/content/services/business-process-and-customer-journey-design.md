Customer experience depends on more than the visible interaction. We help examine the processes, handovers, information, and responsibilities behind a journey so improvements address the whole experience.

## What this can include

- Mapping current customer journeys and operational processes.
- Identifying delays, duplication, unclear handovers, and friction.
- Designing future processes and service interactions.
- Defining ownership and measures for evaluating improvements.

## How we approach the work

We gather perspectives from customers and the people doing the work, map the current experience, and identify opportunities for change. Proposed designs are reviewed against operational constraints and user needs.

## What we work towards

A shared understanding of the intended experience and a practical process design to support it. Changes can then be prioritised, piloted, and refined using feedback.
