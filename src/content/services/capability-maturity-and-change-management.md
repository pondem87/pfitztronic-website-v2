An improvement plan needs to account for both organisational capability and the people affected by change. We help assess the current position, define realistic targets, and organise the steps needed to move forward.

## What this can include

- Capability assessments using agreed criteria and evidence.
- Identification of gaps, priorities, and improvement dependencies.
- Stakeholder engagement, communication, and adoption planning.
- Measures for tracking progress and reviewing the effectiveness of change.

## How we approach the work

We agree on the capabilities and outcomes that matter, gather evidence about the current state, and work with stakeholders to define suitable targets. Improvement actions are sized to the organisation's resources and readiness.

## What we work towards

A prioritised improvement plan with clear ownership and a practical approach to adoption. Progress is reviewed against agreed outcomes so the plan can adapt as needs change.
